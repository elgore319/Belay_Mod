Test bot fixes from playtest feedback. Your game updates to this automatically on the next launch, then asks you to restart.

## Fixed
- **The bot moves more naturally.** It now walks your path a couple of meters behind you at your pace, speeding up when it falls behind and easing off as it gets close. It no longer stares for a few seconds and then lunges, stalls when you move fast, or snaps straight back after a hit.
- **The bot looks where it's going** instead of staring at you through walls. "Look at you" is still in the Bot tab if you want it.
- **Pausing no longer confuses the bot.** It holds still while the game is paused.
- **The bot no longer opens its bag when you open yours.**
- **Blast doors stop the bot.** If a door shuts behind you, the bot is shut out like a player would be. The Bot tab shows "blocked by a door" and **Bring to me** fetches it.
- **Bots can be hurt and killed.** They have the same health as you and slowly heal. Enemy attacks, traps and other hazards that hit a bot hurt it, and at zero health it dies and waits for a revive. Teammates' hammer hits still only knock it back.

## Good to know
The simple bot copies your path, so it can't use items, run out of stamina, get grabbed by barnacles, or be targeted on purpose by enemies. For all of that, use **Bot > Open test client window**, which runs a real second player.
