## Not Alone 0.8.3

- **Better-looking heads on the lore outfits.** Every covered face is now a rounded head instead of a box, with a jaw, chin, nose, cheekbones and ears showing through the cloth. The goggles are now two round lenses in padded cups. The researcher's hood falls in a bell over the shoulders, with a domed, riveted face window. The scavenger's rags are wound around the head with a knot at the back, the expedition parka has a scarf over the chin, and the denizen's skull has a separate jaw and teeth.
- **Not everyone wears a headlamp.** The researcher, pier worker, scavenger and denizen go without one, and their light stays off for teammates too.
- **A more detailed infection.** The Infested look's growths now burst out of tears in the jacket as lumpy clusters with spines and glowing pustules, and veins spread across the jacket from each one. Growth splits the helmet open, and veins run down the face and neck.

No network change.
