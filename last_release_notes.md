A fix for the test bot's second-monitor view. Your game updates to this automatically on the next launch, then asks you to restart.

## Fixed
- **Getting tabbed out to your second monitor.** With the bot's POV shown fullscreen on monitor 2, the game opens a second window there. Whenever the mouse was free (menus, inventory), it could slide onto that window, and a click there moved focus to it. While that view is on, the mouse now stays on your main game window. Alt-Tab still works as normal.
