## Belay 0.6.3

- Bots no longer hold up the ascent or interlude rooms. Once you're inside, a bot that's still outside a few seconds later is brought in next to you, so the room opens.
- Bots can now be hurt by the level's hazards (damage zones and instant-death zones), not only by the mass, enemies' shots and explosions. Hazards take health at the same rate they'd take yours, and a bot that runs out dies and waits for a revive.
- Boxes, crates and other loose props now bump into bots instead of passing through them.
