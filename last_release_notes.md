## Tethered 0.7.41

- **Totem revives rebuild your teammate from the inside out.** When someone is brought back with a totem, their teammates watch it happen: an ember of a heart lights up in the chest, a skeleton grows out of it to the hands and feet, then the body grows back over the bones: strands of raw red muscle knit from joint to joint, pale skin buds and spreads over them in patches, then clothes and gear spread over the skin the same way, while the body cools from a warm glow. The flesh uses the game's own meat texture. It takes about three and a half seconds. The revived player's own view opens from the middle outward through the same ember glow.

No network change.
