## Belay 0.6.24

- **Fixed the hatch instakill.** An interlude's lockdown button (the one that seals the hatch behind you and brings the mass up to it) could be pressed while a teammate was still climbing below, which sealed them out and killed them. Since 0.6.6 it was treated like a door-opening button. Now only buttons that open the way go through freely; anything that closes or seals a door or moves the mass waits until every living player is in the room.

Everyone in the lobby should update.
