First test build of Belay, a co-op mod for White Knuckle (up to 8 players). It hasn't been tested in multiplayer yet, so expect bugs.

## Install
1. Download **Belay-0.5.0-installer.zip** and extract it anywhere (right-click > Extract All).
2. Close the game and double-click **Install Belay.bat**. It finds White Knuckle through Steam and installs BepInEx (the mod loader) and Belay. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**.
3. Start the game and click **Belay (F7)** in the bottom-right of the main menu.

Already manage BepInEx yourself? Use **Belay-0.5.0-mod-only.zip** and extract it into the game folder.

## Updates
Belay updates itself. When a new release is out, it downloads it on launch and tells you to restart the game. There's nothing to redownload. You can switch this off in the Belay menu > Settings.

## Play
- One player hosts a lobby and invites friends through Steam. Everyone needs Belay 0.5.0.
- The host starts the run, and everyone else follows automatically.
- Keys: **F7** menu, **G** revive at a breakroom station, **Tab** switch spectate target.

## What's in it
- Steam lobbies: host public or private, browse, invite
- Shared seed. Everyone follows the host into the run, and late joiners catch up.
- Rope tether between players
- Synced death goo, and doors with a shared countdown before they shut
- Dead players spectate. Revive them at breakroom stations for roaches, paid from your own wallet (currency is never shared).
- Belay menu with Lobby, Players, Rules, Mods and Settings tabs

Uninstall: double-click **Uninstall Belay.bat**.
