## Tethered 0.7.41

- **Totem revives rebuild your teammate from the inside out.** When someone is brought back with a totem, their teammates watch it happen: an ember of a heart lights up in the chest, a skeleton grows out of it to the hands and feet, then the body is built back over the bones layer by layer: raw red muscle, then pale skin, then clothes and gear, each one swelling out of the one under it while the body cools from a warm glow. The flesh uses the game's own meat texture. It takes about three seconds. The revived player's own view opens from the middle outward through the same ember glow.

No network change.
