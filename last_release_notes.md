## Not Alone 0.8.30

- **Biochambers stand against the wall.** Each terminal room's revive pod now sits with its back to a wall near the terminal, its hoses and feed pipes running straight into the wall. Pods only go free-standing when there's no clear wall nearby.
- **Taller biochambers.** The pod is taller, so you can walk out of it standing up.
- **The glass waits for you.** After a revive the pod's glass stays open until whoever was inside has stepped out.

- **The Teeth, the Face and the Hunter are the same for everyone.** The host now runs them and every other player sees the host's: where they are, how they move, leap and burrow, which way they're hunting and what they're doing. Before, each player's game ran its own and they drifted apart.
- **If the host has one and you don't, you get it too.** One that the host's game spawned shows up for teammates who weren't there to trigger it, and one the host's game gets rid of goes for everyone.
- **Hits on the Teeth and the Face count for the team.** Driving them off on your screen drives them off for everyone, and a teammate's hit no longer freezes your game for a moment.
- **The Face no longer speeds up oddly when it goes after a teammate** instead of the host.
- They still bite, hurt and kill whoever they actually reach.
- **Everyone sees the same vent hands.** Each hand goes after the player closest to it, and everyone sees it reach out, grab that player and drag them, arm and all. It doesn't switch to someone else while it's holding a player.

Also new since 0.8.22:

- **The Habitation laser floor no longer blocks teammates behind.** The red security field in the Habitation Labs now runs for each player from their own trigger, so everyone gets the chase. Once it has finished rising it switches off, so a teammate who is still below it (late, revived or respawned) can climb past instead of dying in it.
- **Props a teammate moves can't push you out of bounds.**
