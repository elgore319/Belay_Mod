## Tethered 0.7.41

- **Totem revives rebuild your teammate from the inside out.** When someone is brought back with a totem, their teammates watch it happen: an ember of a heart lights up in the chest, a skeleton grows out of it to the hands and feet, then flesh and gear swell back over the bones while the body cools from a warm glow. It takes about two and a half seconds. The revived player's own view opens from the middle outward through the same ember glow.
