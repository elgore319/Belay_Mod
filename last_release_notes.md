Removes the punch key from 0.5.8. Your game updates to this automatically on the next launch, then asks you to restart.

## Changed
- **No punch key.** F does nothing new anymore. Hitting a teammate with a hammer connects and knocks them back, and it never hurts them.
- Everything else from 0.5.8 stays the same: you can bump into teammates, stand on them, grip and climb them, carry them (they press Jump to get free), and boxes and loose props are shared.

## Still on 0.5.0 or 0.5.1?
Download **Belay-0.5.9-installer.zip** below, extract it and double-click **Install Belay.bat**. Everyone in a lobby needs the same Belay version.
