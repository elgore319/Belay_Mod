## Belay 0.7.26

- **Removed "Pause for everyone".** The PAUSE FOR EVERYONE / RESUME FOR EVERYONE button is gone from the co-op pause menu. Nobody can stop the game for the rest of the team anymore.
- Opening the pause menu in co-op still doesn't stop the game: it only takes your hands off the controls while the world and your teammates carry on.
- Carrying a teammate still drains your grip and slows you down, as in 0.7.20.
