## Not Alone 0.8.32

- **Shooting things in the Nest works for everyone.** Hits on shootable level objects now reach every player: the elevator cable you shoot to drop the elevator, the signal orchids that drop a climbing rope, flashflowers, barnacle tongues, the mass tendrils, and the breakable doors and walls. Whoever shoots it, everyone sees the same thing happen. Players who reach that part of the map later see it already done.
- **The Nest builds the same for everyone.** The first player to reach a new stretch of map shares which levels it got, and everyone else builds those exact levels. Map seeds also no longer drift after a branch (like the Nest's Infested Labs or Hot Zone) is built at different times on different PCs.
- **One way out of the Nest's prison.** Once the team takes the Infested Labs or the Hot Zone, the other way stays shut for everyone, and the run flags those exits set are shared.
- **Everyone gets their own handgun and ammo in the Nest.** Each player has their own handgun and handgun ammo to pick up there.
- **The Mother only kills whoever angers her.** Her anger is shared, so everyone sees her rage, but her crush only kills the player who hit her or crushed the grub. Teammates are spared.
