## Tethered 0.7.38

- **Teammates hold items properly.** Items in a teammate's hands were shown at their full world size and stuck out at odd angles. Now they're sized to fit the hand, and each item is gripped the way it's meant to be: hammers and pipe wrenches by the handle, the machete with its edge forward, and pistols by the grip with the barrel pointing where the player looks.
- **The rapier looks like a sword in a teammate's hand.** It used to show the folded artifact you pick up. Now teammates see a long needle blade with a dark, dripping cup guard and a wrapped grip.
- The scanner ring that every item carries no longer shows up stuck to a teammate's fist.

Everyone in the lobby should update.
