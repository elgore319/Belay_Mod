A fix for getting tabbed out of the game. Your game updates to this automatically on the next launch, then asks you to restart.

## Fixed
- **Getting tabbed out to your second monitor (for example when pressing Space).** The test bot's "POV fullscreen on monitor 2" option opened a second game window on that monitor, which stayed until you restarted and could grab focus away from the game. That option is gone. The bot's POV window (F8) still works inside the game, and for a view on another monitor, open the test client window and drag it there.
