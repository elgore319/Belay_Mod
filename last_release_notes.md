## Tethered 0.7.38

- **Teammates' items sound and look right.** Every sound a teammate's held item makes (gunshots, reloads, swings, pumps, clicks) now plays from where they are. Their shots flash at the hand.
- **Shots and throws land for everyone.** A teammate's bullets, flares, spears and grenades already flew on your screen. Now they also play their impact: sparks, the hit sound, a flare burning where it landed, a spear staying stuck in the wall.
- **Teammates' lights show.** A flashlight, flare or any other lit item lights up around the teammate holding it, pointing where they look.
- **Grenades go off for everyone.** You see, hear and feel a teammate's grenade blast, with camera shake and the shove if you're close. It doesn't hurt you, like every other teammate hit.
- **Lightning and timed effects line up.** Lightning discharges and slow repeating level effects (sparks, bursts) fire at the same moment for the whole team.
- **Gantry turret drones launch on every screen together**, so their flight is shared like other enemies'.
- **Bear traps snap shut for everyone** when one catches a teammate, and open again when it lets go.
- **One driver per seat.** A control seat with a teammate in it doesn't take a second driver.
