## Belay 0.6.31

- **The team rope is now a real rope.** It's simulated like the game's own swinging ropes: it hangs and drapes with gravity, swings, lies on the ground, catches on ledges and corners and slides over them. It's drawn as a proper climbing rope with a woven sheath instead of a flat line.
- **The rope bends round edges.** When the rope runs over a ledge it pulls you toward that edge, not straight through the rock, so a fall over an edge with your teammate gripping above swings you round it.
- **You can grab the rope.** Grab it with your normal grab keys to climb it hand over hand toward a teammate, or swing on it the same way you swing on the game's ropes (sprint, or the game's swing settings). Jumping off keeps your swing's speed. Any rope can be grabbed, not just your own. The bit right next to your own harness can't be, so looking down while climbing still grabs the wall.
- Someone hanging on the rope itself doesn't count as an anchor, so two people can't hold each other up in mid-air.

Rope mode is still off by default (host turns it on in the Belay menu). Nothing new to press. Everyone in the lobby should update.
