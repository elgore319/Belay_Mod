Teammates are now solid, and loose objects move the same for everyone. Your game updates to this automatically on the next launch, then asks you to restart.

## New
- **Solid teammates.** You bump into each other and can stand on each other's heads.
- **Grab each other.** Grip a teammate like a handhold. If you're hanging off them, you ride along as they climb (it costs grip like any hold).
- **Pick each other up.** Grip a teammate while you're standing, or while your other hand holds the wall, and you lift them: they're carried in front of you and follow where you look. Let go while swinging your view to throw them. The carried player presses **Jump** to wriggle free. Holding a wall yourself means you can't be picked up.
- **Hit each other, no damage.** Press **F** to punch the teammate in front of you, or hit them with a hammer. They get knocked back and never lose health. Gripping a wall soaks most of the hit.
- **Shared boxes and props.** Boxes, crates and other loose physics objects move the same on every screen. Whoever holds one controls it (if two people grab the same one, one hand lets go), and when one breaks it breaks for everyone. Items you can put in your bag still work as before.
- Settings: `Teammates > SolidTeammates`, `Teammates > PunchKey` and `World > SyncProps` in the Belay config.
