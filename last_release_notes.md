## Not Alone 0.8.13

- **The nuclear failsafe in the Abyss now happens for the whole team.** When anyone walks into it, every player hears the announcement and, 15 seconds later, sees the blast, the shockwave, the lights switching to emergency, the sky changing and the music, all at the same time. A teammate whose game hasn't loaded that part of the Abyss yet gets it as soon as it loads.

No network change.
