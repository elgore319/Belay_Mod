## Belay 0.6.30

- **You can see what your teammates are doing with their items.** Their avatar now acts it out:
  - swinging a hammer (or any melee item), with a bigger wind-up and strike for a charged swing, and the hammer held back while they charge it
  - setting a piton: pressing it to the wall and knocking it in
  - throwing or setting down an item
  - firing a shooting item
  - eating or taking a consumable (hand to mouth)
  - using any other item (raised out in front)

Nothing new to press; it follows what you already do. Everyone in the lobby should update to see each other's actions.
