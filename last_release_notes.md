## Belay 0.6.6

The ascent (and other interludes) no longer get stuck:
- **Doors and hatches never wait for the team.** Anyone can open the door to the elevator, so a teammate who's behind can still catch up.
- **The elevator is where the team waits.** Its button only works once every living teammate is standing near it, and an elevator that starts when you step in waits until everyone is aboard. The notice says who you're waiting for.
- Bots count too: once you're at the elevator, a bot that isn't is brought along.

Everyone in the lobby should update to this version.
