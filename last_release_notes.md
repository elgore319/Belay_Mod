## Belay 0.6.25

- **Rooms catch up when you get there.** If a teammate pressed a button or set off a scripted sequence in a room your game hadn't loaded yet (you were further down, or dead and waiting for a revive), it used to be lost on your PC, so the room was in the wrong state when you arrived (an interlude's lockdown that never happened, for one). Those are now remembered and played on your PC as soon as that room loads.
- Includes 0.6.24: an interlude's lockdown button waits until every living player is in the room again.
