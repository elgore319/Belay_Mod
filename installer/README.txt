BELAY {VERSION} - co-op mod for White Knuckle

INSTALL
1. Close White Knuckle.
2. Extract this whole zip anywhere (right-click > Extract All).
3. Double-click "Install Belay.bat".
   It finds your game through Steam and installs BepInEx (the mod loader) and Belay.
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" > "Run anyway".
4. Start the game and click "Belay (F7)" in the bottom-right of the main menu.

PLAY
- One player hosts a lobby and invites friends through Steam. Everyone needs Belay {VERSION}.
- The host starts the run and everyone else follows automatically.
- Keys: F7 menu, G revive at a breakroom station, Tab switch spectate target.

UNINSTALL
Double-click "Uninstall Belay.bat". It removes Belay only and leaves BepInEx and other mods alone.
